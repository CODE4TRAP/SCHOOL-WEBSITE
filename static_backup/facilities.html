World-Class Infrastructure For Early Learners

Every corner of Swami Shiwanand Academy & Kid's Play School is meticulously designed to ignite curiosity, inspire creativity, and maintain the highest safety standards for your precious children.

Smart Digital Classrooms

High-definition interactive touch panels, curated audio-visual multimedia modules, educational rhymes, and interactive quizzes that make concepts engaging and vivid.

Montessori Play Station

Vibrant play zone equipped with non-toxic sensory toys, wooden puzzles, role-play corners, mini slides, and ball pool fostering motor skill development.

Safe Transport & GPS

Dedicated school vans and buses covering all major locations of Sasaram and nearby townships with GPS tracking, first-aid kits, and caring female attendants.

Kids Discovery Science Corner

Hands-on exploration kits, safe magnetism experiments, plant germination trays, and magnifying tools that kindle the young child's scientific temper.

Computer & Logic Lab

Ergonomically designed computer lab introducing basic computing, keyboard familiarity, creative digital drawing, and elementary logic through playful games.

24x7 CCTV & Hygiene

High-definition cameras monitoring all corridors, classrooms, and gates. Purified RO water points, sanitized restrooms, and certified fire safety apparatus.

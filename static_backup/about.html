Genesis & Vision

Swami Shiwanand Academy & Kid's Play School was founded with an inspired vision to provide cheerful, value-based foundation education to young learners. Set amidst a lush green, secure campus aloof from urban hustle, our school offers cheerful activity zones, colorful digital smart classrooms, a discovery science lab, and communicative interactive programs.

We believe that early childhood is the foundation of human personality. Our child-centric curriculum combines the best of Montessori play methods and modern experiential learning, ensuring that children develop not only intellectual curiosity but also emotional balance, empathy, and social cooperation.

Our Vision

To nurture independent, joyful, and compassionate learners equipped with sound ethical values and creative curiosity.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, playful, and stimulating learning environment that honors the uniqueness of every child through joyful exploration.

Every child is a unique spark of possibility. Our role as educators and mentors is to fan that spark into a flame of curiosity, self-confidence, and love for lifelong learning.

Aims & Objectives

  • Holistic Development: Cultivating physical, cognitive, linguistic, emotional, and creative skills in every learner.
  • Value-Based Education: Inculcating moral values, respect for parents and teachers, empathy, and patriotism from early childhood.
  • Interactive Smart Learning: Enhancing comprehension through audio-visual smart board modules, educational toys, and storytelling.
  • Child Safety & Comfort: Maintaining 100% child-safe, hygienic, and CCTV-monitored facilities with caring attendants.

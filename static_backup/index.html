About Our School

Swami Shiwanand Academy & Kid's Play School was founded with an inspired vision to provide cheerful, value-based foundation education to young learners. Set amidst a lush green, secure campus aloof from urban hustle, our school offers cheerful activity zones, colorful digital smart classrooms, a discovery science lab, and communicative interactive programs.

Principal's Message

"Education is a shared commitment between dedicated teachers, motivated students, and enthusiastic parents with high expectations. I am honoured and feel very privileged to lead Swami Shiwanand Academy & Kid's Play School. We look forward to working closely with every family to make this early learning experience joyful, holistic, and enriching for every child."

Republic Day Celebrations 2026

Kids dressed as national freedom heroes presented patriotic songs, drills, and tricolor flag performances.

Rangoli & Clay Modeling Contest

Nursery and Kindergarten students showcased their fine motor skills and creative clay figurines with colorful clay.

Independence Day Cultural Fest

Grand celebration with classical kid dance, fancy dress competition, and inspiring words on Indian culture.

Inter-School Kids Sports Carnival

Our young athletes won 12 gold and 8 silver medals in track races, obstacle runs, and yoga demonstrations.

State Level Little Stars Talent Hunt

Students of UKG and Std II achieved 1st rank in regional mental math and spell-bee competitions.

District Art & Craft Honors

Commended for creative recycling crafts and natural color paintings by the district education council.

Go-Green: Tree Plantation Drive

Students planted over 100 herbal saplings across the campus, learning about biodiversity and environment preservation.

Clean Campus & Hygiene Awareness

Interactive session on hand-washing, healthy nutrition, dental care, and zero plastic campus initiative.

Grandparents & Elders Respect Day

A heartwarming program honoring grandparents, fostering cultural roots, gratitude, and moral values.
